# 🤝 Ananta Cloud Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

At Ananta Cloud, we are committed to building a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone—regardless of background, identity, or experience. We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free and safe experience for all.

## Community Values

We strive to foster a collaborative, respectful space by:

* Showing empathy, kindness, and respect to everyone
* Embracing differing opinions, experiences, and viewpoints
* Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully
* Owning our mistakes and learning from them
* Prioritizing the collective well-being of the community

## Unacceptable Behavior

Behavior that will not be tolerated includes:

* Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
* Use of sexualized language or imagery
* Trolling, personal attacks, or political hostility
* Publishing private information without consent
* Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Community moderators are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct and may take action such as:

1. **Correction** – A private reminder or clarification
2. **Warning** – A formal caution with potential consequences
3. **Temporary Ban** – Suspension from participation for a period of time
4. **Permanent Ban** – Removal from the community for repeated or severe violations

## Reporting Issues

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it by contacting us at **[abuse@anantacloud.com](mailto:abuse@anantacloud.com)**. We commit to investigating all reports promptly and confidentially.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, including GitHub repositories, social channels, events, and any official Ananta Cloud representation.
